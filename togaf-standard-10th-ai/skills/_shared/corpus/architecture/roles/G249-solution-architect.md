# 解决方案架构师 / Solution Architect

记录：`G249:role:solution-architect`；依据：G249；内容角色：`source-grounded-role-analysis`。

范围：具体新建/变更解决方案及其过渡架构。

架构领导/治理、专业范围或协作关系不自动表示人事直属汇报；岗位、角色、任务分别建模。

学历、工作年限、认证、法定签字权等未在相应证据给出时保持未规定，不能从技能等级推造。

## 角色定位

- 围绕明确需求设计、治理并交付新增或变更能力，形成可实施的过渡架构。（G249 PDF 第 23 页；原文：responsible for the delivery of new and/or changed capabilities）

## 职责

- 明确服务/流程/组织/运营模型及技术变化，并考虑生产运营维护演进（G249 PDF 第 24 页；原文：Specifying changes to services, processes, organization structure, and operating models as well as technology, including the planned operation, maintenance, and evolution of the solution within a production environment）
- 保证现有和计划构件兼容相关架构、战略、政策和标准（G249 PDF 第 24 页；原文：Ensuring that existing and planned solution components are compatible with relevant architectures, strategies, policies, standards, and practices）
- 全面纳入安全、隐私与测试要求（G249 PDF 第 24 页；原文：Taking full account of the requirements for security, privacy, and testing of solutions）
- 说明不同部署拓扑和构件选项的收益与问题（G249 PDF 第 24 页；原文：Clearly identifying the benefits and issues associated with different solution topologies (such as with on-premise and off-premise solutions and services), systems, sub-systems, and components for each change being specified and implemented）
- 创建并推动实施目标路线图及组成的过渡架构（G249 PDF 第 24 页；原文：Creating, evolving, and ensuring the implementation of the Target Architecture Roadmaps, for each major product and/or service change and its constituent transition architectures）
- 以风险治理支持实施并管理变更与偏离（G249 PDF 第 24 页；原文：Providing guidance and risk-based governance to support solution implementation, including managing requests for changes and any necessary deviations from specifications）
- 促进利益相关者沟通协作并达到目标服务水平（G249 PDF 第 24 页；原文：Ensuring that all stakeholders can communicate and collaborate effectively to deliver the targeted solutions and services and their expected service levels）

## 决策与治理权限

- 监督解决方案的规格、开发与实施，落实适用性、效率、合法性及相关标准。（G249 PDF 第 24 页；原文：development, and implementation of new and changing solutions）
- 提供风险驱动的实施治理，管理变更请求及必要的规格偏离。（G249 PDF 第 24 页；原文：risk-based governance）

## 任职与能力要求

- 具备丰富经验与专业成就。（G249 PDF 第 23 页；原文：experienced and accomplished professionals）
- 充分参与业务/技术及其他利益相关者沟通，理解关注点、需求和期望。（G249 PDF 第 24 页；原文：fully engaging with all business/technology and other stakeholders）
- 能全面考虑安全、隐私及测试要求。（G249 PDF 第 24 页；原文：security, privacy, and testing）

## 输入与输出

- 输入具体需求、上位目标、战略政策及服务水平期望；输出系统/子系统/构件及互操作设计、方案路线图和过渡架构。（G249 PDF 第 23 页；原文：specific requirements）
- 设计覆盖生产环境的运营、维护和演进安排。（G249 PDF 第 24 页；原文：planned operation, maintenance, and evolution）

## 分析结论

解决方案角色把上位架构约束与具体需求转为可实现变更，负责协作、取舍与落地治理，不能缩减为代码或产品设计。

### 等级定义

- 1 / Background：Some understanding but reliant on specialists.
- 2 / Aware：Understands and can address issues with the support of specialists.
- 3 / Knowledgeable：Good detail enabling effective decision-making, requiring specialist input in difficult situations.
- 4 / Expert：Extensive experience, practical, and applied knowledge.
