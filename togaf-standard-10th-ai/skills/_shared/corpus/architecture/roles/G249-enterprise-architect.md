# 企业架构师 / Enterprise Architect

记录：`G249:role:enterprise-architect`；依据：G249；内容角色：`source-grounded-role-analysis`。

范围：跨业务/技术分段的企业全景。

架构领导/治理、专业范围或协作关系不自动表示人事直属汇报；岗位、角色、任务分别建模。

学历、工作年限、认证、法定签字权等未在相应证据给出时保持未规定，不能从技能等级推造。

## 角色定位

- 发起、领导并交付跨企业的重大业务与技术变革，为跨分段架构提供一致性和治理。（G249 PDF 第 19 页；原文：creating, leading, and delivering significant technical and business change）

## 职责

- 把业务/技术战略和目标转为运营模型变更（G249 PDF 第 20 页；原文：Translating business and technology strategies and objectives into related changes in the operating model）
- 评价当前能力及所需能力变化（G249 PDF 第 20 页；原文：Assessing current capabilities and identifying required changes in those capabilities to achieve business and technology strategies and objectives）
- 描述人员、组织、服务、流程、数据、信息、技术与环境的相互关系（G249 PDF 第 20 页；原文：Describing the interrelationships (between people, organization, service, process, data, information, technology, and the external environment) needed to enable the delivery of effective products and services）
- 创建和演进企业未来状态的架构模型与视图（G249 PDF 第 20 页；原文：Creating and evolving architectural models and views embodying the key principles that describe the enterprise’s future state, and that enable its successful evolution across the Architecture Landscape）
- 定义、沟通并实施支持迭代与敏捷的架构工作实践（G249 PDF 第 20 页；原文：Defining, communicating, and implementing Enterprise Architecture working practices to support and enable iterative and Agile working）
- 识别并沟通架构约束、原则和政策（G249 PDF 第 20 页；原文：Identifying, documenting, and communicating the constraints, principles, and policies necessary to define, assure, and govern the effective evolution of the Architecture Landscape (its environment of services implemented in its various components)）
- 领导、辅导分段与解决方案架构师（G249 PDF 第 20 页；原文：Developing, leading, enabling, and supporting the Segment and Solution Architects working within the architecture team through leadership, mentoring, and coaching）
- 传达变革驱动因素并参与架构治理（G249 PDF 第 20 页；原文：Providing awareness of relevant change drivers to the enterprise and participating in the governance activities associated with the changing Architecture Landscape）

## 决策与治理权限

- 通过信息系统对整体业务服务交付承担责任。（G249 PDF 第 19 页；原文：accountability for overall business service delivery）
- 定义塑造架构全景的原则/政策，并监督架构变更治理。（G249 PDF 第 19 页；原文：define principles and policies）

## 任职与能力要求

- 在若干关键技术或业务领域具备熟练掌握能力。（G249 PDF 第 19 页；原文：mastery in several key technical and/or business areas）
- 具有重大交付经历以及与关键利益相关者合作的记录。（G249 PDF 第 19 页；原文：track record of significant delivery）
- 工作范围不限于单一解决方案侧面或分段。（G249 PDF 第 19 页；原文：not limited to any particular solution aspect or segment）
- 能指导和辅导他人。（G249 PDF 第 19 页；原文：coach and mentor others）

## 输入与输出

- 输入企业战略、目标、当前能力及变革驱动；输出运营模型变更、未来架构模型/视图及架构工作方式。（G249 PDF 第 20 页；原文：Translating business and technology strategies and objectives）
- 输出约束、原则和政策，支持架构全景演进及治理。（G249 PDF 第 20 页；原文：constraints, principles, and policies necessary）

## 分析结论

EA关注跨分段的一致性、互操作性、架构原则与治理；技能矩阵强调广度和架构判断，同时保留领域专家协作。

### 等级定义

- 1 / Background：Some understanding but reliant on specialists.
- 2 / Aware：Understands and can address issues with the support of specialists.
- 3 / Knowledgeable：Good detail enabling effective decision-making, requiring specialist input in difficult situations.
- 4 / Expert：Extensive experience, practical, and applied knowledge.
