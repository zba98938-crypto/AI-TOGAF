## 1.3 为什么需要本手册

架构师应评价企业目标，识别平衡、集成的能力集合，有效实现目标。企业有竞争目标及需创建、集成、演进的多种能力方案；EA为企业内外适当变更奠基。有些方案必须上线即符合法规，有些约束少，可在可用基础上持续改善。

选择生命周期和实践前，先评价变更性质、风险和完整性。图1展示大企业可能遇到的变化画像及相对变化量。

图1：企业变化画像。稳健变更示例为核电站、飞行控制、核心银行，完整性和风险较高；敏捷变更示例为销售活动、异步消息、信息文档共享，完整性和风险较低；快速变更示例为市场窗口只剩两周，完整性和风险不确定。真正企业敏捷，是按每项变化的目标、需求及方案，选择最适画像，不是一种方法适合所有。

最敏捷企业能够支持全部画像。一般敏捷实践可用于三者，但特别适于最常见的中间类型。架构师职责是促成可实施于业务和信息系统的构建块及模式；指导其创建演进；帮助其他架构师设计者决策解冲突。工作始终在需求与约束、可选组件、方案集成、新技术能力采用及交付途径间取舍。

本书基于规模化敏捷与架构经验，帮助恰当应用，既避免不断快速失败却不成功，也避免分析设计瘫痪延迟有价值实施。

## 2.1.1 思想与概念

敏捷实践者把架构视为重要决策，关注规划及变化活动，而非全景中组件、子系统、系统性质结构组成。关键是并非所有决策都要预先作出，应保留选项，延至最后负责任时点。

途径依赖语境需求：一些需预判驱动，一些需适应驱动。过程及其演进要素都应按企业目标需求运行、预判、调整，保持全景及组件灵活性。在高度敏捷组织，这表现为：

- 有意架构Intentional：以有目的声明、模型、决策表达期望未来状态，规划战略举措，提高产品质量属性、促进跨团队设计实施同步。原文随后误写International，按本段概念保留为源文问题。
- 涌现架构Emergent：开发交付中形成高层模式原则，架构要素从团队工作中出现。基于实际实现影响客户满意，为修订有意指导提供技术证据，支持演进迭代。

敏捷导向组织常偏爱涌现，因而重视自组织和局部决策多于组织塑形与协作决策。

G226:p0014:b005：2.1.1 
Ideas and Concepts 


G226:p0014:b006：Agile practitioners describe architecture work as the important decisions you need to make. Agile 
approaches focus on planning and change activity, rather than the nature, structure, and 
composition of the components, sub-systems, and systems that make up the Architecture 
Landscape of the enterprise. A defining characteristic of Agile-style Architecture Landscape 
management is recognizing that all decisions do not need to be made upfront. Options should be 
preserved, and decisions delayed, until the last responsible moment. 


G226:p0014:b007：The selection of approach should be one of an understanding of context and requirements. There 
will be some change that needs to be anticipation-led and some that needs to be adaptation-led. 
The challenge for both – to create an evolving enterprise’s Architecture Landscape, and flexibility 
in the components, sub-systems, and systems within that landscape – is therefore that the process 
and the elements evolved by the process operate, anticipate, and adjust in line with the goals, 
objectives, and requirements of the enterprise. 


G226:p0014:b008：When Enterprise, Segment, and Solution Architects work in heavily Agile-style organizations, this 
relationship between anticipation and adaptation is reflected in the following two classes of 
architecture: 


G226:p0014:b009：• 
Intentional Architecture: expresses the desired effects of the architecture, consisting of a 
purposeful set of statements, models, and decisions representing some future architectural 
state 
International architecture also provides a set of planned architectural strategies and 
initiatives, which enhance product architecture quality attributes and promote inter-team 
design and implementation synchronization. 


G226:p0014:b010：• 
Emergent Architecture: architecture elements that emerge from the solution or product 
delivery team during development, as higher-level patterns and principles are established 
from the development effort in progress 
Being derived from actual implementation, emergent architecture directly affects 
customer satisfaction and delight and provides practical technical evidence to reframe the 
intentional architecture guidelines in support of a fully evolutionary and iterative 
implementation approach. 


G226:p0014:b011：In more Agile-focused organizations, it will often be assumed that emergent architect approaches 
are preferable to more heavily intentional ones. Consequently, more emphasis will be given to 
self-organizing teams and localized decision-making than organization shaping of teams and 
collaborative decision-making.

## 2.1.2 以TOGAF进行敏捷规划

敏捷源于并仍根植软件开发，近来与长期业务适应概念结合于EA。既看未来问题机会指标，也看基线和历史绩效，驱动预判。

Barry Boehm在1981年正式指出软件工作的发现性质使早期估算、草图精度变化很大；DeMarco 1982《控制软件项目》及2003《与熊共舞》确认并提出估算质量因子。较复杂或超出少数人产品规模的项目，估计可能有2—4倍差异，可高估四倍也可低估四倍，后者似更常见。脚注1：Barry Boehm资料见https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Barry_Boehm。

来源：PDF物理页 15；G226:p0015:b005, G226:p0015:b006, G226:p0015:b007

G226:p0015:b005：2.1.2 
Agile Planning with the TOGAF Standard 


G226:p0015:b006：Agile planning began and is still significantly founded in the arena of software development. More 
recently, the long-running concept of business adaptability has been more closely linked to the 
concepts emerging from Agile planning. Both come together within the framework of Enterprise 
Architecture. A key element in this perspective is looking forwards based on indicators that sense 
future issues and opportunities, as well as understanding the current baseline and performance in 
the past. This drives the anticipation aspects embedded in Agile planning. 


G226:p0015:b007：One of the key concepts behind Agile thinking is a problem identified formally by Barry Boehm 
back in 1981.1 Boehm identified that when developing software, the discovery nature of the work 
meant that there was significant variation in the accuracy of estimates and solution sketches at the 
early stages of change. DeMarco in Controlling Software Projects [DeMarco 1982] and Waltzing 
For Bears [DeMarco 2003] confirmed these observations and promoted the concept of an 
estimating quality factor for change estimates. The variation associated with an estimating quality 
factor for any relatively complex project or larger size (bigger than a product developed by just a 
few people) is around a factor 2 – 4. You can be four times too large in your estimate just as you 
can be four times too small (although in practice the latter seems to be more common).

## 2.1.2.1 不确定锥与TOGAF

图2用敏捷熟悉的不确定锥说明TOGAF整合敏捷的框架。了解接受变化的准备度、识别并准备处理问题，可降低锥顶不确定性，准备度评估见标准。

本文将探索的变化在TOGAF称愿景、敏捷称产品；成功取决于能力集合交付令赞助者/用户满意的结果。两者均为期望而非现实，随外部环境、内部能力和知识增加持续评审，必要时重构。

脚注2：变化程度依性质而异。多数IS方案组合既有、复用、新组件；熟知组件使期望接近最终实现，适配少；完全新软件解决新问题时，初始可能粗略，需逐步增强原型几乎完成后才充分认识方案。多数变化偏前者，但专业新产品和客户部署可有显著新颖性。

## 4.3 结对架构

来源借用结对编程：驾驶者开发、观察者评审，两者频繁交换角色。将此方式用于架构，可较早发现错误、形成共识，改善模型一致性、质量并加快交付。

观察者评审时还考虑战略方向、改进思路与未来潜在问题，使驾驶者专注当前建模任务，获得安全网和引导。因此驾驶者可以是建模专家，观察者可以是另一建模者、专题专家或决策者；异地也能结对，适合分布团队或稀缺专家。双方交换编辑责任；经验表明早期发现错误可抵消结对成本，并促进知识传递和相互学习。

G226:p0043:b007：4.3 
Pair Architecting 


G226:p0043:b008：Pair architecting is an Agile technique where the driver develops the code and the observer reviews 
the code. The two programmers switch roles frequently. 


G226:p0043:b009：Architecting in pairs, following the pair program technique, will improve consistency, accelerate 
delivery, and improve the quality of the architecture model since mistakes are discovered earlier 
and there is an early consensus on the architecture. 


G226:p0043:b010：While reviewing the architecture models created, the observer also considers the strategic direction 
of the architecture, coming up with ideas for improvement and likely future problems to address. 
This frees the driver to focus all of their attention on the modeling aspects of the current task, 
using the observer as a safety net and guide. 


G226:p0043:b011：This allows the modeler to be an expert modeler and the observer to be a fellow modeler, topic 
expert, or a decision-maker. 


G226:p0043:b012：Pair architecting also allows the participants to be at separate locations which is very beneficial
