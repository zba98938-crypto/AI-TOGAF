## 本文档

原文：This Document。PDF 物理页：[5, 6]。状态：`source-compared-revised`。

本文档是《构建块的选择方法》TOGAF® 系列指南，由 The Open Group 制定并批准。更多信息、工具、指南和其他资源见 www.opengroup.org/architecture。

本指南的目的与结构：

本文档与简短的速查文档《Choosing TOGAF® Building Blocks》（《选择TOGAF构建块》，见 https://www.opengroup.org/library/g248）配套使用；速查文档提供便于日常工作的易用形式。本指南更详细地解释如何为变革活动配置构建块，以及该方法背后的理由。

本文档分为四个主要部分：

- 第一部分包括第3章《为各变革画像的相关实体选择构建块》，描述需要识别并适应的不同变革类型和形态。
- 第二部分包括第4章《本指南的方法》，描述如何为每类变革配置视点。
- 第三部分包括第5章《使用TOGAF ADM驱动变革》、第6章《建立语境》、第7章《勾勒概念》、第8章《配置变革》、第9章《变革画像的检查清单与治理》、第10章《实现物理（及部分逻辑）交付物》、第11章《概念实现——确保收益》。这一部分描述在TOGAF架构开发方法（ADM）周期中如何组织并交付变革：建立变革语境；识别需要变更的相关要素；组织交付单元、实施方法和计划；治理变革的实施与部署。
- 第四部分包括附录A《按域划分的架构制品》、附录B《架构交付物》和附录C《TOGAF标准的裁剪》，相应涵盖按域划分的架构制品、架构交付物，以及TOGAF标准的裁剪。

更多信息、工具、指南和其他资源见 www.opengroup.org/architecture。

## 关于TOGAF系列指南

原文：About the TOGAF® Series Guides。PDF 物理页：[7]。状态：`source-compared-revised`。

TOGAF® 系列指南说明如何使用TOGAF标准，以及如何调整它以满足具体需要。

系列指南预期是TOGAF标准中发展最快的部分，定位为标准的指导部分。TOGAF基础内容预期保持长期稳定，而使用指导可以针对行业、架构风格、用途和问题。例如，支持扩展企业转型所需的利益相关者、关注点、视图和支撑模型，可能与将内部IT环境迁移到云所需的内容显著不同；两者都使用ADM，都从架构愿景开始，开发目标架构，并走向实施与迁移计划。TOGAF基础内容仍是跨行业、领域和风格的必要支架。

About the TOGAF® Series Guides

## 企业中的变革画像

原文：2. Change Profiles in an Enterprise。PDF 物理页：[14, 15, 16, 17, 18]。状态：`source-compared-revised`。

企业架构为企业内部及企业之间的集成变革提供基础。有些情况下，这可能需要稳健的方法，确保解决方案及构成解决方案的构建块满足大量监管与法律要求，并且必须在部署时一次成功。另一些情况下，解决方案与构建块可能约束很少，可以有可变但可用的实现，并随时间改进，同时处理不同层面的政治、社会、个人与技术债务。

在识别了与企业当前语境、状态与愿景（使命、驱动因素、关注点、目标、目的与需求）以及变革粒度相关的变革性质后，可以识别出不同且更或较适当的变革类型。图 2-1 展示了在相当规模的企业中可能遇到的典型变革画像，反映在决定变革生命周期与实践之前必须考虑的不同风险与完整性关注。

图 2-1：企业变革画像

三类宽泛的变革画像为：

- **快速**（Rapid，短时间交付驱动）——简单组件的近乎即时实现（如快速应用开发 RAD 等扩展原型）；
- **功能**（Functional，可实现能力驱动）——针对特定有界功能与时间的组件快速迭代（如联合应用开发 JAD、动态系统开发方法 DSDM、敏捷技术）；
- **稳健**（Robust，风险与架构驱动）——复杂、风险更高、规模更大的组件的长期交付，往往需要跨企业互操作、覆盖企业或企业一部分的广度（如采用 COBIT®、SABSA®、Prince 2® 或成功项目群管理 MSP 等方法的管理型项目/项目群）。

多数变革情境中，即便不是更多，也会有大量过渡采用**功能**画像实现；偶尔需要纯粹的速度，由**快速**画像支持；某些变革则需要谨慎的**稳健**画像方法。不同变革画像需要不同水平的“有意架构”与“涌现架构”以及治理。在任何规模的企业中、任何具有一定复杂度与规模的特定变革中，这三种画像很可能都相关。架构师与产品/项目/变革负责人应协作、动态地按最合适的画像管理变革。不同构建块可能因每次变革的具体需求与性质而异（本文档提供特征性实体集，标识不同变革画像可考虑的构建块类型）。

企业架构规划与发现式技术的融合，为在更广泛的演进与互操作整合需求中，通过小型、有针对性的变革单元涌现并检验直接的客户价值提供了语境。快速画像适用于必须快速发生、且负面效应风险较高的变革；功能画像适用于需要以标准交付节奏交付真实能力（但可容忍各种政治、社会、个人或技术债务）的变革；稳健画像适用于功能与非功能需求必须完全合规、风险必须最小化的变革——应用于复杂且规避风险的变革，如核电站、飞机控制系统与药物研发（非极端紧急时）。

TOGAF 标准描述基线架构、目标架构与过渡架构，分别反映当前状态、期望的最终状态，以及向总体目标状态推进的可实施中间解决方案组件单元，如图 2-2 所示。这描述了不同架构视点及其使用之间的关系，以响应企业目标与目的，实施新能力与解决方案。

图 2-2：TOGAF过渡架构的实体关系图（ERD）

由于 TOGAF 标准是通用框架，不依赖且包容行业流行趋势，它不强制规定架构全景变更的任何特定方法，而是期望不同企业与情境可能需要采用当时可用的不同方法。如今，原型式变革与敏捷产品开发方法再次普遍（如同 20 世纪 80 年代与 90 年代初 [Gilb 1988; Arthur, 1992]）。

支撑这种增量交付方法的敏捷概念包括**最小可行产品**（Minimum Viable Product，MVP）与**最小可市场化产品**（Minimum Marketable Product，MMP）。它们运用长期以来的考虑：快速收集反馈、从学习中迭代与增量开发很重要；并不总能详细定义目标状态与更远的过渡状态。这些敏捷概念可与 TOGAF 标准方法对齐，如图 2-3 所示。

图 2-3：含MVP和MMP概念的TOGAF过渡架构实体关系图

当变革开始时存在若干未知因素，通常无法给出相当完整的目标描述。TOGAF 标准建议在变革向目标推进时采用“愿望式递进”（aspirational progression），但目标会随该推进而变化。

当情境涉及业务与架构全景变更时，企业可能不确定真实客户需求，必须提出假设并验证。若内部与外部条件（如技术、政治、竞争对手）变化过快且随机，可预测的变革视野就会受限。然而，即便特定变革相关条件确实变化，也可能存在稳定要素，使变革得以推进，并且是变革有任何成功机会所必需的。

企业架构的作用是驱动变革、确保解决方案的企业级思考，并支持适当的迭代与增量（有些小而短，有些大而长），以反馈塑造架构全景。战略与企业架构的职能，是反映“有意方向、强制变革与涌现能力”之间平衡的现实。

在小规模变革中，**市场质量产品**（Market Quality Product，MQP）可作为基线与目标之间的中间状态，甚至作为目标架构。架构需求可按支持交付 MVP 并实施适当非功能需求的方式排定优先级。架构应提供适当手段，把变化中或新产品集成到现有架构全景，不允许各类债务积累过多。可创建适当架构视图，利用架构模式与正确选择的标准简化过程。MVP 可能是产品路线图中的临时步骤，在灵活性与速度之间平衡，并在适当时避免过度架构。

在大规模变革中，可能并行交付多个 MVP。已交付的 MVP 会改变架构全景，架构师的职责是确保全景不散乱，并向有效状态演进。部分决策可下放给较低层级团队，但架构全景仍应作为整体管理。

应对气候变化的考量，是“意图”与“涌现”（也称“规划”与“发现”）两种方法之间所需更复杂“共舞”的典型例子。应对气候变化需要制定长期战略，而该战略必须回应正在出现的气候威胁，以及尚未完全知晓的技术与政治解决方案；同时，又必须围绕 30–50 年的目标尽快组织行动。我们从目标状态（净零排放，甚至负排放）的高层架构愿景开始，同时也有一个由许多相互依赖要素构成、十分复杂的基线架构。尽管需要立即行动，但考虑不周的行动会浪费资源，甚至使情况更糟；过早对关键组件做出承诺，会限制后续可做的事情。过渡状态既要在每个 MVP 内部处理技术变化（及其他变化）的可能性，也要处理 MVP 之间的变化。对于复杂变革，需要在随时间演进的集成架构与每个已实施的 MVP 之间取得平衡。这使未来的过渡更容易从每个基线架构向演进中的目标架构推进。本质上，这是把愿景中描述为目标的总体概念，经由使能能力，最终落实为已实施的 MVP。

选择早期构建块以应对气候变化，其困难可由交通领域说明。若要越来越多地使用电动交通，我们可能需要易于更换的电池（目前尚未提出）、建在道路等出行环境中的感应供电（目前尚未提出），或所有人都能及时使用的充电点（目前进展过慢，且很少考虑那些没有空间安装自有充电设施的人——例如住在没有停车位的公寓中的人）。对构建块及支撑基础设施的规划与投资十分昂贵，且需要数十年才能实现。然而，该基础设施必须与交通模块（汽车、货车、飞机、船舶等）集成并交互，而且要以一致、稳定的方式进行。这些交通模块持续变化，有效标准又很少，因此推进构建块与集成环境的组合绝非易事。

在这些以及其他许多情境中，现实是我们必须找到有意架构与涌现架构的正确结合。我们不能只快速构建孤立组件，也不能把组件限制在长期计划所预测的范围内。但我们确实可以把两种方法结合起来，以实现最大有效性，并得到更好而非更糟的架构全景。

2. Change Profiles in an Enterprise

**G248:p0014:c001 · PDF 14**

Enterprise Architecture provides a foundation for integrated change within and between enterprises. In some cases, this may require robust approaches, ensuring that the solutions and the building blocks that make up the solution meet many regulatory and legal aspects and must work first time on deployment. In other cases, the solutions and the building blocks may have few constraints and can have variable but functional implementations that improve over time, navigating different levels of political, social, personal, and technical debt.

**G248:p0014:c002 · PDF 14**

Having identified the nature of the change related to an enterprise’s current context, state, and vision (its mission, drivers, concerns, goals, objectives, and requirements) and change granularity; different more or less appropriate types of change can be identified. Figure 2-1 shows characteristic profiles of change likely to be encountered across a reasonably large enterprise. They reflect the different risk and integrity concerns that must be considered before deciding on change lifecycles and practices.

**G248:p0014:c003 · PDF 14**

Figure 2-1: Enterprise Change Profiles

**G248:p0014:c004 · PDF 14**

The three broad change profiles are identified as:

**G248:p0014:c005 · PDF 14**

• Rapid (short time delivery-driven) – Near immediate implementations of simple components (e.g., extended prototyping such as Rapid Application Development (RAD)

**G248:p0015:c000 · PDF 15**

• Functional (implementable capability-driven) – Fast cycles of component delivery for specific bounded functionality and time (e.g., Joint Application Development (JAD), Dynamic System Development Method (DSDM), Agile techniques)

**G248:p0015:c001 · PDF 15**

• Robust (risk and architecture-driven) – Longer term delivery of complex, riskier, larger scale components, often interoperable across different enterprises, the breadth of an enterprise or part of an enterprise (e.g., managed projects or programs using approaches such as COBIT®, SABSA®, Prince 2® or Managing Successful Programs (MSP))

**G248:p0015:c002 · PDF 15**

In most change situations there will be a significant, if not a greater number of transitions, implemented using the functional profile. Occasionally pure speed is needed. This is supported by the rapid profile. In some changes, careful robust profile approaches will be needed. The different change profiles require different levels of intentional and emergent architecture and governance. In any enterprise of scale and in any specific change of some complexity and scale, it is likely that all three of these change profiles will be relevant. Architects and Product/Project/Change Leaders should collaboratively and dynamically manage the changes in line with the most appropriate profiles. Different building blocks may be needed for the specific requirements and nature of each change (this document provides characteristic sets of entities that identify the relevant types of building blocks that may be considered for the different change profiles).

**G248:p0015:c003 · PDF 15**

The blending of Enterprise Architecture planning and discovery style techniques provides a context for the emergence and testing of direct client value using small, targeted units of change within the broader need to evolve and integrate solution components in an interoperable manner. The rapid profile provides an approach when change has to happen quickly with a higher risk of negative effects. The functional profile provides an approach when change needs to deliver real capability at standard delivery pace, (but can tolerate various forms of political, social, personal, or technical debt). The robust profile provides an approach when functional and non-functional requirements must be fully compliant, and risk must be minimized. This approach should be used for complex and risk averse change such as that to nuclear power stations, aircraft control systems, and drug development (when not in time extremis).

**G248:p0016:c000 · PDF 16**

Figure 2-2: Entity Relationship Diagram (ERD) of a TOGAF Transition Architecture

**G248:p0016:c001 · PDF 16**

The TOGAF Standard describes Baseline, Target, and Transition Architectures. These reflect the current state, the desired final state, and the implementable units of intermediate solution components that progress towards an overall target state as shown in Figure 2-2. This describes the relationships between the different architecture viewpoints and their use, to implement new capabilities and solutions in response to an enterprise’s goals and objectives.

**G248:p0016:c002 · PDF 16**

Because the TOGAF Standard is a general framework, agnostic to and inclusive of prevailing trends in the industry, it does not mandate any particular approach to this Architecture Landscape change, expecting that different enterprises and situations may necessitate different approaches to be used from those available at any point in time. Today, prototyping style change and Agile product development approaches are again common (as they were in the 1980s and early 1990s [Gilb 1988; Arthur, 1992]).

**G248:p0016:c003 · PDF 16**

Among the Agile concepts in support of this incremental delivery approach are Minimum Viable Products (MVP) and Minimum Marketable Product (MMP). Those apply the long-used considerations that it is important to gather feedback rapidly and develop iteratively and incrementally from learning, and it is not always possible to define in detail the target and more distant transition states. These Agile concepts can be aligned to the TOGAF Standard approach, as shown in Figure 2-3.

**G248:p0017:c000 · PDF 17**

Figure 2-3: ERD of TOGAF Transition Architecture with MVP and MMP Concepts

**G248:p0017:c001 · PDF 17**

A reasonably complete target description is often not possible when there are several unknowns at the start of a change. The TOGAF Standard suggests the use of aspirational progression as the change moves towards the target but the target changes as that movement occurs.

**G248:p0017:c002 · PDF 17**

When the situation is associated with business and Architecture Landscape change, the enterprise may not be sure what real customer needs are and have to hypothesize and then validate those hypotheses. If internal and external conditions change too quickly (e.g., technology, politics, competitors) and randomly, this restricts the change horizon that can be predicted. However, when conditions associated with any specific change do vary, there may also be stable elements that can enable change to progress and are required for it to have any chance of success.

**G248:p0017:c003 · PDF 17**

The role of Enterprise Architecture is to drive the change, assure enterprise-wide level thinking of solutions, and support for appropriate iterations and increments (some of which may be small and short, others may be large and long), with feedback shaping the Architecture Landscape. It is the function of strategy and Enterprise Architecture to reflect the reality of this balance between intentional direction, enforced change, and emergent capability.

**G248:p0017:c004 · PDF 17**

In small scale change a Market Quality Product (MQP) can be used as an intermediate state between baseline and target or even as a target architecture. Architecture requirements may be prioritized in such a way as to support delivering a MVP with suitable non-functional requirements implemented. Architecture should provide appropriate means to integrate changing or new products with an existing Architecture Landscape and not allow too much of the various types of debt to accumulate. Appropriate architecture views can be created to ease the process using architecture patterns, and

**G248:p0018:c000 · PDF 18**

properly selected standards. An MVP may be a temporary step in a product roadmap, balancing between flexibility and speed, and avoiding over-architecting, when appropriate.

**G248:p0018:c001 · PDF 18**

In large change, there may be several parallel MVPs delivered. Delivered MVPs modify an Architecture Landscape and the architect’s role is to assure the landscape is not scattered and evolves towards an effective state. Some of those decisions may be delegated to lower-level teams, but nevertheless the Architecture Landscape should be managed as a whole.

**G248:p0018:c002 · PDF 18**

Consideration of the response to a changing climate is a good example of the more complex dance needed between the approaches of intention and emergence (also known as planning and discovery). Dealing with a changing climate requires that a long-term strategy has to be shaped that must respond to emerging climate threats and technological and political solutions that are not yet fully known, but for which action must be soon marshalled in relation to a 30-50 year target. We start with a high-level architecture vision of the target state (net zero emission, or even negative emission). We have also a Baseline Architecture, which is complicated with many interdepended elements, while immediate action is required, ill thought-out action wastes resources and may make things worse. Commitments to key components made too early limit what can be done later. Transition states need to address the potential for technology change (and other change) within each MVP as well as between MVPs. With complex change there is a need to balance the integration architecture over time with each implemented MVP. This enables future transitions to be progressed more easily, from each Baseline Architecture towards the evolving Target Architecture. Essentially this is developing a general concept described in the vision as goals, through enabling capabilities and ultimately to implemented MVPs.

**G248:p0018:c003 · PDF 18**

An example of the difficulty associated with selecting early building blocks to address climate change is that of transport. If we are to increasingly use electric transport, we may need infrastructure such as easily replaceable batteries (not currently proposed), induction power delivery built into travel environments such as roads (not currently proposed), or accessible and timely charging points available to all (currently progressing too slowly with little thought for those who have no space for their own charging capability – such as those living in flats with no parking). Planning and investment into the building blocks and the supporting infrastructure is expensive and requires many tens of years to realize. However, that infrastructure must integrate with and interact with the transport modules (cars, lorries, planes, ships, etc.) and do so in a consistent and stable way. Those transport modules change continuously and there are few effective standards such that progressing the combination of the building blocks and the integrated environment is no easy matter.

**G248:p0018:c004 · PDF 18**

The reality in these and many other situations is that we have to find the right blend of intentional and emergent architecture. We cannot just rapidly build isolated components nor restrict components to those predicted in a long-term plan. We can, however, blend the two approaches together to achieve the greatest effectiveness and better, rather than worse, Architecture Landscapes.

## 建立语境

原文：6. Establishing the Context。PDF 物理页：[28, 29]。状态：`source-compared-revised`。

“建立语境”聚焦表 5-1 的步骤 1 与 2。企业/分段/解决方案架构师（统称 ESS 架构师）评估变革的目标与目的，并识别一组平衡且集成的能力、构建块与解决方案，以有效实现期望成果，满足请求变革的利益相关者。

实施变革的前两步与 TOGAF ADM 阶段 A 对齐：通过识别企业当前状态及其愿景（通常体现为使命、驱动因素与关注点，以及派生的目标、目的与需求）来建立语境。随后把它们与可能影响的三个特定架构粒度层级相关联：

- **战略架构**——为运营与变革活动提供组织框架，支持高管层的方向设定；
- **分段架构**——为运营与变革活动提供组织框架，支持项目群或组合层的方向设定与有效架构路线图开发；
- **能力架构**——为变革活动提供组织框架，在组件规格与解决方案实施层面实现能力增量的有效架构路线图开发。

大型企业中，不同类型的架构师在这三个层级工作（通常在总体企业架构框架内以团队或专业实践协作）；较小组织中，一位架构师可能承担多个甚至全部角色。

- **企业架构师**倾向在战略层工作，在广泛企业范围创建路线图、参考模型、指南与最佳实践；
- **分段架构师**倾向在组合或项目群层工作，为相关组合/项目群创建路线图、参考模型、指南与最佳实践，并监督与其分段所管能力相关的更详细构建块、子系统与系统变更；
- **解决方案架构师**倾向在能力与解决方案/组件实施层工作。

他们有时在由企业与分段架构师塑造的更广泛语境（更高层架构）中工作，有时直接与其他特定变革利益相关者对接。他们规定、塑造并治理与实施相关的变革，确保纳入变革的能力被正确转化为合适的构建块、组件、接口、子系统与系统，并交付期望的整体成果（通常以服务水平绩效描述）。

## 勾勒概念

原文：7. Outlining the Concept。PDF 物理页：[30, 31]。状态：`source-compared-revised`。

按表 5-1 的步骤 3 与 4 执行“勾勒概念”。语境建立后，接下来两步确立用于处理变革的具体概念，同样与 TOGAF ADM 阶段 A 对齐。

需要一组绩效期望或度量来代表委托利益相关者期望的成果。在概念步骤中，通常以 KPI 形式记录：描述要实现什么、何时实现、期望的绩效水平（应具体、可度量、可行动、可靠且有时限，即 SMART）。KPI 应在识别变革目标概念之前勾勒，并常随可能解决方案实现的浮现而逐步修订。

许多情况下会存在更高层架构（变革必须在其内发生的企业、分段或解决方案/能力层架构）。它在语境步骤中建立，并提供变革推进所应遵循的约束集。

变革概念开发的输入承接语境开发所识别的内容，并增加更具体的需求与约束。对语境及这些附加要素的评估，使支持变革所需的特定能力集得以识别。理解这些能力后，可开发可能解决方案及其主要构建块的草图，使所有利益相关者能就“草图是否可能回应其关注点”形成意见。概念步骤生成该愿景与 KPI，必须由相关利益相关者评审并批准（或否决）。

可能解决方案及相关构建块，可用各企业对 TOGAF 企业元模型（图 3-1）的专门化，以适合变革性质、愿景深度与利益相关者理解的详细程度来描述。应纳入的元模型要素将在下一节“配置变革”中详细讨论与建议。开发概念时，常使用这些构建块及其交互的高层草图形式。

形成一致概念所需时间取决于变革性质及期望解决方案的范围与触达。较长的例子是商用核聚变反应堆的稳健变革画像语境与概念：探索团队已工作 20 年，仍在探索相关构建块及其集成应是什么、KPI 的现实值可能是多少；尚未能进入实际可运营、可市场化解决方案应包含什么（无论逻辑还是物理）；尚未获得各利益相关者对“存在逻辑解决方案规格依据”的批准，更遑论物理解决方案实施。该变革一直无法推进并完成逻辑解决方案定义，仍处于语境与概念开发阶段。较短的例子是 X（原 Twitter）被 Elon Musk 收购后的变革：新语境与新概念迅速建立，并推进了向在线运营产品实施变革；虽然属于更偏功能的变革画像（招致许多人认为重大的政治、社会、个人与技术债务），但已在数月内走完概念实现，并经历多次实施变革迭代。

一旦语境与概念获相关利益相关者同意，变革即可进入该受管变革单元完整构建块与组件集的一组或多组逻辑定义。这些逻辑解决方案步骤可表示为 MVP，并可向 MMP 推进。

The KPIs should be outlined before identifying the target concept of the change and will often be gradually revised as the possible change solution implementation emerges.

**G248:p0030:c004 · PDF 30**

In many cases there will be a superior architecture (an Enterprise, Segment, or Solution/Capability- Level Architecture within which the change must happen). This will have been established in the context steps and provides a set of constraints within which the change should progress.

**G248:p0030:c005 · PDF 30**

The inputs to the development of the change concept follow on from those identified in context development, adding more specific requirements and constraints. An evaluation of the context and these additional elements enables a specific set of capabilities to be identified that will be needed to support the change. Once those capabilities are understood, a sketch of the possible solution and its main building blocks can be developed to enable all stakeholders to generate an opinion on whether the sketch is likely to address their concerns. The concept steps generate this vision and the KPIs, which must be reviewed and approved or otherwise by the relevant stakeholders.

**G248:p0030:c006 · PDF 30**

The possible solution and associated building blocks can be described using each enterprise’s specialization of the TOGAF Enterprise Metamodel (see Figure 3-1) at a level of detail that makes sense for the nature of the change, the required depth of the vision, and the understanding of the stakeholders. The elements of the metamodel that should be considered will be discussed and suggested in detail in the next section on “Configuring the Change”. When developing the concept, a high-level sketch form of these building blocks and their interactions will often be used.

**G248:p0030:c007 · PDF 30**

The time taken to develop an agreed concept depends on the nature of the change and the scope and reach of the desired solution. A longer example is the robust change profile context and concept of a commercial Nuclear Fusion Reactor. Teams exploring this possibility have been working for 20 years but are still exploring what the relevant building blocks and their integration should be and what the realistic values for the KPIs might be. They have not yet been able to move to what the actual operable and marketable solution should contain; either logically or physically. There is no approval yet from the various stakeholders that there is a basis for a logical solution specification let alone a physical solution implementation. This change has been unable to move forward and complete the logical solution definition and is still in the context and concept development stages. A shorter example is the changes made to the social networking service X (formerly known as Twitter) since the takeover by Elon Musk. A new context and concept were quickly established, and progress made on implementing change into the live operational product. While this change is of the more functional change profile

**G248:p0031:c000 · PDF 31**

(incurring what many people consider is significant political, social, personal, and technical debt) it has moved all the way through to conceptual realization within a few months and many iterations of implemented change.

**G248:p0031:c001 · PDF 31**

Once the context and concept are agreed by the relevant stakeholders a change can move into one or more sets of logical definition of the complete set of building blocks and components for that unit of managed change. These logical solution steps can be represented as an MVP that can be progressed towards an MMP.

《TOGAF 标准——应用 ADM》[C220]：

> “ADM 是用于架构开发的通用方法，旨在处理大多数系统与组织需求。然而，通常需要修改或扩展 ADM 以适配特定需求。应用 ADM 之前的任务之一，是审查其组件的适用性，然后根据个别企业的情况进行适当裁剪。该活动很可能产生一个‘企业特定’的 ADM。”
